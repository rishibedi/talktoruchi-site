The Community

Backing onto Sixteen Mile Creek

Five Oaks sits at 1303 Dundas Street West in Oakville, on a rare freehold site that directly borders the protected Sixteen Mile Creek Natural Heritage System. Homeowners get permanent ravine and greenspace views that can't be built out — a genuine rarity this close to the Dundas Street corridor.

The community is being brought to market by Caivan Communities, sold through RARE Real Estate Inc., with roughly 140 homes planned across towns and detached lots. Pricing, floor plans, and the deposit structure have not been publicly released yet — VIP-registered buyers get first access before the public sales launch.
